# Decisions

One line per decision, newest last. Decisions listed in `docs/PROMPT.md` are final and
not repeated here.

| Date | Decision | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-10-09 | Phase plans are written as checklists directly, without the interactive plan mode | Sessions run unattended; plan mode would block on approval nobody can give. |
| 2026-10-09 | TypeScript pinned to 5.9.x, ESLint 9.x | TypeScript 7 / ESLint 10 are too new for typescript-eslint and the React plugins. |
| 2026-10-09 | Camera presets selected through the URL query `?preset=<name>` | Lets Playwright screenshot a preset with no UI automation. |
| 2026-10-09 | Three.js units are metres; all house data is in centimetres and converted at scene build time | Three/R3F lighting and physics defaults assume metres; plans are dimensioned in cm. |
| 2026-10-09 | Orientation: plan sheet "up" = north. Party wall (isolation phonique) at y = 724; terrasse, balcon and openings 004/005/006/010/012 at x = 0 = west (élévation OUEST, width 724); entry door 002, couvert and 009 at x = 1055 = east (élévation EST); stair window 007 at y = 0 = south (élévation SUD, width 1055 + 500 couvert). Scene: +X east, −Z north, so the sun sits toward +Z. | The three elevation titles and widths fix all four sides without a north arrow. |
| 2026-10-09 | Twin villas are stacked along plan y; the neighbour starts above y = 724. The "3.35 m²" cartouche on page 4 is the neighbour's and is excluded from rez.json | Located by plans-reader at x ≈ 1470, y ≈ 745-800, beyond the party wall. |
| 2026-10-09 | geometry-extractor writes `src/data/house/<level>.json` itself and reports a summary | Keeps 100+ lines of JSON per level out of the main thread; the main thread still reviews and commits. |
| 2026-10-09 | Exterior wall thicknesses differ per level (sous-sol 18-20, rez 30-40, étage 24-49 cm, deduced from dimension chains) and are kept as extracted | Interior polygons match printed m² with these values; phase 2 will judge the façade alignment visually and may unify the thickness there, not in the data. |
| 2026-10-09 | Subagents render plan pages with PyMuPDF; `Read` with `pages` fails in their shell (pdftoppm not on the agent PATH) | Observed in all three extractions; the fallback is already documented in the agents. |
| 2026-10-09 | CHAMBRE 2 is modelled as an L-shape including its 47 × 102 cm entrance nook (12.17 m² vs 11.24 printed, 8.3 %); tolerance 10 % for that room only | The rectangle alone is already 11.69 m²; leaving the nook out of every room would leave a hole in the floor behind the hall door. |
| 2026-10-09 | Interior geometry: walls are boxes split around openings (piers, sill, lintel), floors and slabs are extruded polygons with stairwell holes, stairs are straight flights along a `Stair.ascent` vector; pure functions in `src/scene/*.ts`, thin R3F components | No CSG dependency, everything unit-testable, light on Iris Xe. |
| 2026-10-09 | Stairs: 16 risers sous-sol → rez, 14 rez → étage (section p. 9 numbers 12-30), straight flights ascending toward plan +y | The winder layout is not readable at this scale; a straight flight in the right stairwell is enough for navigation and will be refined if a photo check demands it. |
| 2026-10-09 | Room labels are canvas-texture sprites with constant screen size, not drei `Html` | `Html` dropped one of six portals under React 19 StrictMode; sprites need no DOM and no font download, so Playwright and Chrome render the same thing. |
| 2026-10-09 | Room presets: `<room>` = diagonal view from the corner nearest an interior door, `<room>-2` = from the far side toward the widest glazed exterior opening; exterior bays never count as entrances | Five visual-check rounds: door-to-centroid views faced blank walls, the séjour diagonal crossed the stairwell, and bays were mistaken for doors. |
| 2026-10-09 | Carrelage floors get a procedural 60 cm tile grid and wall boxes thin edge lines until phase 4 textures arrive | Flat-shaded empty rooms were unreadable for visual-check (a corner view of the salle de bains read as "pressed against a wall"). |
| 2026-10-09 | Shell levels: terrasse −17, couvert slab −14 with roof +251/+285.5 on three 20 cm posts at its east edge, balcon slab +251/+280 with 100 cm glass balustrade on three edges and a 120 cm cover at +536/+565.5, roof slab +535/+560, acrotère 15 cm thick to +606 | Read from elevations p. 6 and 8 by plans-reader; post count beyond the sheet edge and acrotère height are estimates. |
| 2026-10-09 | Sky: Poly Haven `kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky` 1k HDRI (CC0) served through Vite `publicDir: "assets"`, drei `Sky` fallback when the file is absent; sun from a NOAA solar model for 46.24° N 7.41° E, default June 15:00 | Keeps a fresh clone renderable without assets and makes shadows follow the real orientation. |
| 2026-10-09 | Exterior rooms (terrasse, couvert, balcon) keep their room polygons for labels and presets but draw no floor; the shell draws their slabs | One source of truth for their outline, correct levels for their slabs. |
| 2026-10-09 | Canvas uses `preserveDrawingBuffer: true` and the ready flag is reset on every canvas mount | Headless captures came out as the page background on some runs; the cost on Iris Xe is negligible. |
| 2026-10-09 | Glass is a plain transparent `meshStandardMaterial`, no transmission | Transmission renders the scene a second time: a minute per frame under SwiftShader and a real cost on Iris Xe. |
| 2026-10-09 | Catalogue models: Poly Haven glTF at 1k (sofa_02, sofa_03, modern_arm_chair_01, wooden_table_02), multi-file, restored through the API include map; no bed exists on Poly Haven, so the procedural bed is the bed | Poly Haven is CC0 across the site and its models are real-world scale; Kenney kits looked too toy-like for the realism target. |
| 2026-10-09 | Material library is procedural (colour, roughness, optional tile grid) under stable ids; PBR texture sets can replace them later under the same ids | Scene files and /deco proposals reference ids, not files, so textures can arrive without touching scenes. |
| 2026-10-09 | Scenes are saved by the browser through a Vite dev middleware (`PUT /api/scenes/<name>`), not downloads | Keeps `scenes/*.json` on disk as the single source of truth that `/deco` and git see; the production build keeps PNG export only. |
| 2026-10-09 | Editor state in zustand with whole-scene snapshots for undo / redo (50 steps) | Scenes are small; snapshots are simpler and safer than inverse operations. |
| 2026-10-09 | Room wall finishes apply to partitions and to a 1 cm inner skin on exterior walls; the façade keeps its render colour | One box per wall piece cannot carry two materials; the skin follows the opening cut-outs for free. |
| 2026-10-09 | `?ui=0` hides the panels; the screenshot script always uses it | Panels were covering a quarter of every verification shot. |
| 2026-10-09 | /deco: the vision step is the only agent call; matching and layout are deterministic code (`src/deco/`) driven by placement hints (window, cardinal walls, far wall, centre, corner, beside / in front of) | Reproducible, unit-testable, and the proposal JSON stays editable by hand between two runs. |
| 2026-10-09 | Ceiling pendants are skipped by `/deco` (logged), not turned into floor lamps | No ceiling fixture geometry yet; a floor lamp in the room centre was worse than nothing. |
| 2026-10-09 | Parquet materials are plank textures (120 × 18 cm, staggered); tiles stay square | Square tiles read as tiles even when brown. |
| 2026-10-09 | Interior light: HDRI (intensity 1.0) + sun + warm ambient + warm neutral hemisphere fill, ACES tone mapping at exposure 1.05 | visual-check measured shaded walls at 166-190 with a blue cast under the HDRI alone; the neutral fill brings them to 205+ with B−R under 6 while the exterior stays correctly exposed. |
| 2026-10-09 | Orbit mode renders on demand, walk mode continuously; the roof slab only in the all-levels view | Saves the laptop GPU when idle; the roof z-fought with the étage ceilings. |
| 2026-10-11 | Library materials point to Poly Haven PBR sets (`pbr`: `full` = colour map × tint, `relief` = normal + roughness on the material colour); every box, rounded box and cylinder gets box-projected UVs in metres; the flat colour stays the fallback while loading or when a file is missing | One texture set serves all paint colours and fabric dyes; metric UVs keep planks, weave and plaster at real scale on any piece. |
| 2026-10-11 | Tone mapping Khronos Neutral at exposure 1.0 instead of ACES 1.05 | ACES desaturated the bright finishes (beige-pink walls read cream, jute read pale); Neutral keeps material colours as specified. |
| 2026-10-11 | Interior walls lose their outline edges; the façade keeps them | Textured walls read as walls; black outlines looked like a CAD drawing. |
| 2026-10-11 | `/deco` keeps the base kitchen, dining set, desk and wardrobe unless the proposal brings its own, and lays new pieces out around them; doors keep a clear zone over their full width (sliding doors included); wall decor avoids openings and tall furniture | An inspiration image of a salon says nothing about the kitchen; the first séjour run put the sofa against the 3.2 m sliding door and frames over glazing. |
| 2026-10-15 | GitHub Pages enabled through the API (`build_type=workflow`); the earlier deploy failed with 404 only because Pages was never switched on | The build job was green; `deploy-pages` needs the site to exist first. |
| 2026-10-15 | Stairs are half-turn stairs (`Stair.halfTurn`: treads of flight A, winders, flight B, side of flight A, central wall): steps are plan polygons with a sloping 18 cm waist, winders fan at equal angles around the central wall end | Plans p. 3-5 and section p. 9 all show two 91 cm flights, a 15 cm central wall and 6 winders at the south; solid blocks would have filled the headroom of the flight below. |
| 2026-10-15 | Rez: plâtre partition `wall-entree-cuisine` (8 cm, 120 long, y 452) plus the kitchen island close the entrée from the kitchen; the entrée is an L-shaped corridor (4.49 m²) | Plan p. 4 at 300 dpi and photo IMG_5498 (narrow corridor, coat hooks on the partition); the first extraction had read the entrée as open. |
| 2026-10-15 | Guard lines on stairs (`Stair.guards`): white stringer up to the treads plus black bar balustrade 90 cm above them, heights read from the tread under each post | The séjour side of the étage flight is open on p. 4 and photo IMG_5501 shows the black bars following the slope. |
| 2026-10-15 | "Réaliste" quality = N8AO (half resolution) + bloom + SMAA + Neutral tone mapping via @react-three/postprocessing, soft PCF shadows 2048; `?quality=low` or the panel turns it off | Ambient occlusion is the single biggest realism gain indoors (corners, furniture contact); the laptop keeps a fast mode. |
| 2026-10-15 | Ceiling fixtures from the photos (`src/scene/fixtures.ts`), one shadowless point light per fixture group when "Lampes allumées" is on; sky, HDRI and fills fade with the sun elevation | Up to ~20 point lights without shadows stay affordable; a lamp per spot would not. |
| 2026-10-15 | Joinery: PVC frames white inside / anthracite outside, granite sills, entrance door with three glazings, interior doors standing open at 90° with white casings; dark wood skirting 7 cm interrupted at floor-level openings | Photos IMG_5498-5510. |
